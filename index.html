WHY CAMPUSHUB?

Complaints shouldn't disappear.

CampusHub creates a clear connection between students who report problems and the people responsible for resolving them.

📝

Easy Reporting

Report problems such as damaged facilities, electrical issues, water leaks and other campus concerns in one place.

👀

Public Visibility

Students can see existing complaints and check their current status without having to repeatedly ask about them.

🔐

Controlled Access

Students must log in before submitting complaints, helping prevent misuse by people outside the campus.

HOW IT WORKS

From report to resolution.

01
🔑

Login

Students and authorized administrators access CampusHub through a common login system.

02
📝

Report

A student submits a complaint with the necessary details such as category, location and description.

03
🔍

Track

The complaint becomes visible on the issue board where students can follow its progress.

04
🛠️

Resolve

Authorized administrators update the complaint status as the issue is reviewed and resolved.

TRANSPARENCY

Know what is happening after you report.

Instead of wondering whether a complaint was noticed, students can follow its progress directly through CampusHub.
